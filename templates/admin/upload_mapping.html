Two supported formats

1. Roster only (recommended)

Just the students — no seat planning needed. Auto-allocate from the session page afterwards. Add an optional exam_time column if different subjects in the same shift run at different times (e.g. MCA at 9:30–11:30, iMCA at 9:30–12:30) — it prints on the hall ticket.

roll_numbernamesubject_codeexam_time
21CS045A. MenonMCA9:30–11:30 AM
21CS046R. KumariMCA9:30 AM–12:30 PM

Leave exam_time blank to just show the session's shift on the ticket instead.

2. Pre-set seating (advanced)

Add hall_number, row, seat and each row is seated exactly where you specify — the hall number must already exist in the target session. exam_time works here too.

hall_numberrowseat
101C14

Either way: rows with a roll number already imported for this session are skipped and reported after upload — nothing is silently overwritten.
